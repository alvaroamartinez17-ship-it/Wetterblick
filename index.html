Your trail rules

Fitted to your 179 rides on Kappelberg, Korb and Schorndorf (2024–2026). Dry days the trails need after a heavy rain (a light shower needs proportionally less), and the gust speed from which riding in the forest is too dangerous.

Learn from my rides

Load a file with your riding days: date and place, or date with latitude/longitude, or a Strava activities.csv. The app downloads the rain history for each place and finds the drying times that match when you actually rode. Rides without a place count for the place shown at the top.

How it works: every hour of rain fills a “trail wetness” store (full after about 12 mm). It empties at the speed of your drying rule, adjusted by how much the sun and wind actually evaporate that day compared with the seasonal normal (FAO evapotranspiration). Your scale: 1 Wet = store above 4 mm. 2 Wet firm = 2–4 mm. 3 Werksboden = the first days after the store drops below 2 mm (up to 3 days in April–September, 5 in October–March) while less than 10 mm has evaporated. 4 Dry = after that. 5 Dry loose = after about 25 mm of evaporation without rain. Storm safety: “Don’t ride” when gusts reach your limit (default 60 km/h, Beaufort 7–8) or the DWD has a storm, thunderstorm or snow-break warning for this place. Gusts from 45 km/h and the 24 hours after a storm (48 hours after gusts of 75 km/h or a severe warning) are marked with ⚠ because of falling branches and fallen trees. Past rain: DWD station measurements via Bright Sky, gaps filled from the DWD ICON analysis. Future: DWD ICON forecast via Open-Meteo.
